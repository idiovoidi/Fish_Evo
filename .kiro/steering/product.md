# Product Overview

Fish Evolution Simulation is an interactive aquatic ecosystem simulator focused on evolutionary processes and natural selection. Users observe how fish with heritable genetic traits (size, speed, metabolism, color) adapt and survive through breeding, mutation, and environmental pressures.

## Core Features

- **Evolution & Genetics**: Fish inherit traits from parents with mutation chances, demonstrating natural selection over time
- **Ecosystem Dynamics**: Simple food chain with fish consuming food, sharks as predators, and population tracking
- **Observer Role**: Users primarily watch the ecosystem evolve, with limited interaction (manual food drops, entity inspection)
- **Emergent Behavior**: Complex patterns emerge from simple individual entity rules

## Key Mechanics

- **Hunger & Energy**: Fish must consume food to survive; starvation leads to death
- **Breeding**: Mature fish reproduce when conditions are met, passing genetic traits to offspring
- **Predation**: Sharks hunt fish, creating selective pressure
- **Ambient Life**: Crabs, seaweed, coral, and debris populate the underwater environment
